FUNCTIONAL FIX CONTRACT v5 (xr-options-fix, from the Pro 2 session of 2026-11-06; supersedes the matching lines above):
Scope. `scope` in a snapshot is the scope shown. The header's choice is kept apart (OptionsMenu.scope_choice): a tab with rows in only the other scope shows those, and a scope press on it says so in the footer. No tab is blank because of the scope it was reached in. `Global rows filtered to workspace scope; selected rows filtered to monitor scope` still holds for the rows shown.
Keyboard. Ownership is plain binds in the default submap (view.OPTIONS_BINDS, the placement panel's bind table), sent as `binds_key MODS CODE`, not the key bind panel's submap: Hyprland checks a bind's submap for mouse buttons too, so inside it the left button and wheel binds stop. Printable keys are not taken: internal character controls remain the text path. The worker in ViewControl._menu_bind_work is the only writer of that table, off before on.
Lifecycle. Moving the dot keeps the menu up; 30 s without input closes it. A press on empty space or another handle closes the menu and carries on; a press on a desktop passes through and restores typing, the free dot's as well as the real cursor's. Maximize, a VR video and the placement panel close it. A cursor on a desktop under the menu turns into the free dot, as for the help card.
Completion. A receipt alone is still not completion. A change with no owner callback stays pending for at most PENDING_TIMEOUT_S (5 s), then the footer states what is known: `Sent; nothing reports back for this` if the renderer took every frame, otherwise error `No confirmation from the owner`.
Slider. A held slider previews locally; the owner hears once, on release, against the current revision, with the row checked by id, monitor and scope.
Geometry. Header ranges and tab cells are kOptions* in options_geometry.hpp, drawn from those constants and mirrored in options_geometry.py. Next > goes to the first tab of the next page, the last page back to the first, by mouse and keyboard.

RESTORE GEOMETRY AS IT STANDS (recorded for the placement mockups, 2026-11-06; xr-options-fix does not move it). Defined by options_maximize(tile,true) and options_restore_rect in options_geometry.hpp, s=level_bar_scale(tile): 26*s square, x = level bar x - 38*s (kept inside the tile, 16*s from its left and 42*s from its right), y = tile top + 16*s, moved down only as far as the view's edges require. Hit region: the drawn square plus 4*s all round, tested on the native mesh (maximize_geometry hit_mesh). Hint: 84*s x 20*s, 32*s under the button's top. It sits inside the captured picture, so while the cursor is in the hit region the app gets no pointer: the cursor is parked and the click is the run's (mode 'restore'). Restore, the Step-Back Band (Cody's pick, R3, 2026-11-07; replaces the inset Restore and lifts the v3 "no excursion pointer" rule for this route only): nothing is drawn over a maximized picture. Pressing the pointer against the output's top edge and resting there opens the band. The rest (`tools/native_spike/restore_band.py`): the outermost row touched (0.5 logical px), then 350 ms with the hand within 2 logical px; it survives 14 px down (EdgePush's 12 px nudge, under a desktop placed above) and starts over on any bigger move, so sliding along the edge never opens it. Leaving the zone cancels, a press restarts it, a held button blocks it (the non-consuming click binds stay on while maximized; each app click there costs one bind helper process), as does a hand's pan, typing, a video or the key panel. Open: the picture eases back to 84% about the view's centre in 220 ms (`kRestoreStepScale`, `kRestoreStepMs`); a picture already leaving 46 px above it keeps its size and only the pill comes; the existing grab hides and parks the cursor and the dot takes over at the picture's top edge, moving in canvas px by relative motion, free of the output, with no hook and no plugin change. Restore is a 122 x 26 pill 12 px above the picture, hit region 4 px round it, the renderer's mesh in `maximize_geometry` (`rect`, `hit_mesh`, new `picture`, always as fully stepped back). Press and release on it restores. Back in: 8 canvas px inside the picture brings it forward, and the app gets the cursor on the same column, 24 logical px under the edge, and no rest counts until it has left the zone. A press off Restore shuts the band and is swallowed by the grab. Ten seconds with no motion shuts it. Ctrl+Alt+Down, a desktop change and losing the geometry all shut it.
